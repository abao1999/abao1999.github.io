I am a Ph.D. researcher in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Texas at Austin, advised by [William Gilpin](https://www.wgilpin.com/) and [Sanjay Shakkottai](https://sites.google.com/view/sanjay-shakkottai), and affiliated with the [NSF Institute for Foundations of Machine Learning](https://www.ifml.institute/). I work at the intersection of **generative modeling** and **dynamical systems** to bring forth novel perspectives on the mechanisms underlying modern foundation models, and to design improved neural surrogates for multiscale physical systems.

Previously, I studied physics at [Caltech](https://www.caltech.edu/) (B.S. 2021). I had the incredible fortune to explore many topics under the mentorship of inspiring and supportive faculty. I briefly worked on: generative priors for computational imaging with [Katie Bouman](https://computationalcameras.org/); geometric learning for high-energy physics with [Harvey Newman](https://www.pma.caltech.edu/people/harvey-b-newman) and the CERN Compact Muon Solenoid (CMS) collaboration, and with [Corey Adams](https://coreyadams.net/about.html) at Argonne National Lab; quantum algorithms with [Austin Minnich](https://www.minnich.caltech.edu/) at the Caltech Institute for Quantum Information and Matter; and astrophysics and robotics at the NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL).
