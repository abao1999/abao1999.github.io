I contribute to [cphy](https://github.com/williamgilpin/cphy), the open-source materials for a graduate course in computational physics taught by [William Gilpin](https://www.wgilpin.com/) at UT Austin.

## Topics

- Numerical integration and numerical linear algebra
- Optimization
- Sampling and Monte Carlo methods
- Statistical learning
- PDE solvers

Course notebooks and lecture materials: [github.com/williamgilpin/cphy](https://github.com/williamgilpin/cphy).
